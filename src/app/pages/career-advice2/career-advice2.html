Career Advice

Tips to grow your developer career

Bite-sized advice to help you level up, one idea at a time

Master Git and debugging tools

Knowing how to bisect bugs, write clear commit messages, and use your browser’s debugger efficiently saves more time than typing fast ever will.

Understand the business impact

Great developers don't just write code; they solve user and business problems. Always ask *why* a feature is being built before deciding *how* to build it.

Write tests for your future self

Automated tests aren't a chore—they are safety nets. They give you the confidence to refactor and push changes without breaking existing functionality.

Embrace constructive code reviews

Feedback on your pull requests isn't a critique of your intelligence. Treat code reviews as a free learning opportunity and a way to elevate team standards.

Improve your soft skills

Technical chops get you hired, but empathy, active listening, and clear written communication are what make you a senior engineer people want to work with.

Learn to step away when stuck

Staring at a bug for hours often yields diminishing returns. A short walk or a good night's sleep allows your brain to find solutions passively.

Document as you go

Good documentation saves hours of onboarding and troubleshooting for your team. If you struggled to figure something out, write down the fix for others.

Prioritize work-life balance

Burnout is the biggest threat to a long software engineering career. Pace yourself, set clear boundaries, and maintain hobbies outside of technology.
